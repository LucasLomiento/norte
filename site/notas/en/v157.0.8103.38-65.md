- **Chromium 157.** Norte moves from Chromium 155 to 157.0.8103.38, bringing the security fixes of both versions.
- **Signed releases.** Packages and the pacman database are now signed with a GPG key. If you installed before this and your `pacman.conf` says `SigLevel = Optional TrustAll`, pacman asks once whether to import the key: answer yes and everything keeps working. For full verification, follow the [README](https://github.com/LucasLomiento/norte-releases#readme).
- **More ways to install.** A `.deb` for Debian 12+, Ubuntu 24.04+ and derivatives, which updates from the notice at the bottom of the sidebar, like the pacman package.
- **Also:** new AI features that came with Chromium 157 stay switched off, and Norte now opens on systems without journald, such as containers and distrobox.
