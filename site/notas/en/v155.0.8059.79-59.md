- **Dragging a tab out of the window on Hyprland:** the tab no longer turns into a window (or splits the screen) as soon as the pointer leaves the window, before you let go. It follows the pointer until you drop it: on empty space it becomes a window; over another Norte window it goes into it (or splits the screen, if you drop it on the page). What happened: when the pointer passed over some programs (Zed, for example), Hyprland told Norte the tab had been dropped.
- **Split view through Hyprland's drag:** with the screen already split, the card showing where the tab will go appears in the right place on the side panes too; and when you leave the window over the page, it goes away (before, it stayed, and dropping anywhere split the screen).
