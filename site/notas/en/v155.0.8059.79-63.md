- **Folders inside folders** in the sidebar's pinned sites, up to 5 deep. Drag a folder onto the bottom half of another, or use "Mover para a pasta" (move to folder) and "Nova pasta aqui dentro…" (new folder inside) on right-click. Nested folders show up by their path ("Trabalho › Clientes") in the menus, including the tab's "Fixar no espaço" (pin to space). A closed folder still shows the active tab's site, even if it's in a folder inside it. Importing from Zen brings its nested folders.
- **Drag a tab from the list into the pinned sites:** drop it between sites (it goes there), on the bottom half of a folder (into the folder), on the favorites box or tiles, or on the line under the pinned sites (to the end). The tab becomes the pinned site and leaves the list. With nothing pinned yet, a "Solte aqui pra fixar" (drop here to pin) area shows up at the top while you drag.
- **Double-clicking a folder no longer renames it** (opening and closing it quickly turned into renaming): each click opens or closes it, and renaming a folder is on right-click, "Renomear pasta…" (rename folder). On pinned sites and tabs, double-click still renames.
- **Claude extension, Norte style:** while it's using the browser, instead of the black "debugging" bar at the top of every tab, an orange card shows at the bottom of the sidebar, "Claude está usando o navegador" (Claude is using the browser), with "Parar" (stop) to disconnect it. Other extensions that use the debugger keep the bar (it's a safeguard).
- **Claude's container:** the tabs Claude opens can always go to the same container (Settings → Containers → "Container do Claude"), so it uses your work logins even while you're in your personal space. You can also switch it from its card in the sidebar. The default is still the container of the space you're in.
