- **No Google suggestions in the address bar:** what you type in the address bar no longer goes to the search engine with every letter, and the list no longer shows the searches it suggests. What's left is the search for what you typed, history, bookmarks, commands and the calculator, and the search engine only gets the search when you press Enter. To get the suggestions back, turn them on in Norte Settings → Privacy (Privacidade) → Search engine suggestions (Sugestões do buscador).
