- **The hidden address bar (`Ctrl+Shift+S`) disappears completely:** it takes the bookmarks bar and the window buttons with it, and the page goes all the way to the top. Touching the top of the page with the pointer (or leaving the window across the top) brings everything back, like the hidden sidebar; it hides again when you go back to the page, and stays while you type in the address.
- **The sidebar shows only the window's space:** no headers for groups from other spaces, not even in the middle of a drag.
- **Dragging a tab to another window's sidebar** puts the tab in the space that window is showing (the tab keeps its container). Dragging the last tab out of a space keeps the window on that space, with a new tab.
- **`Ctrl+Shift+T` no longer piles up "New tab"s**: when a tab comes back to the space, the leftover empty ones go away.
