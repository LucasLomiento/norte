- **Collapsing and expanding the sidebar (`Ctrl+S`) like resizing the window:** the page changes width along with the sidebar, frame by frame, and the site reflows during the motion. Before, it stayed still, jumped all at once at the start or the end, and flickered while it redrew.
- **Update without leaving Settings:** in General (Geral) → Updates (Atualizações), "Check now" ("Procurar agora") shows the new version and turns into "Update now" ("Atualizar agora"). The download percentage, the password in the system window and "Restart now" ("Reiniciar agora") all appear right there. Before, you had to close Settings and use the notice at the bottom of the sidebar, which now shows the percentage too.
- **A restart notice after updating from the terminal:** with Norte open, a `sudo pacman -Syu` that brings a new version makes "Updated — restart to use version X" ("Atualizado — reinicie pra usar a versão X") appear at the bottom of the sidebar and in Settings. "Restart" ("Reiniciar") closes and reopens every window, with its tabs, including the ones hidden in other workspaces. Before, a forgotten window kept Norte on the old version with no notice at all.
