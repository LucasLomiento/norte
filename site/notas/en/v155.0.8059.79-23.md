Navegador is now called **Norte**. If you already had it installed, you don't need to do anything: the update changes the name on its own and brings your profile along (logins, spaces, containers and extensions move from `~/.config/navegador` to `~/.config/norte`), and the `[navegador]` section of `pacman.conf` keeps working. The command is now `norte`.

- **Hidden address bar:** `Ctrl+Shift+S` (or Settings → Appearance, "Aparência"). `Ctrl+L` and the new tab show the bar while you type.
- **Split screen from the keyboard:** `Ctrl+Alt+V` side by side, `Ctrl+Alt+H` top and bottom (the same shortcut again undoes it), `Ctrl+Alt+X` swaps the sides. Dragging a tab to the edge of the page also splits, now on Hyprland too.
- **Spaces:** closing the last tab of a space no longer takes you to another space, and `Ctrl+W` on the last tab doesn't close the window; a new tab takes its place. Moving a tab to another window and back no longer makes the space's other tabs vanish (if you had "missing" tabs, you'll see them again). "Continue where you left off" brings back the tabs of the space in use again.
- **KDE and GNOME:** you can drag the window by the empty part of the sidebar (double-click maximizes), and the hidden sidebar opens when the pointer touches the edge even when the window isn't focused.
- **1Password:** the extension talks to the 1Password app with no setup.
- The system volume control shows "norte" instead of "chromium".
