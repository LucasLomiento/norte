- **Split view with up to 4 tabs, like Zen.** Drag a tab from the sidebar onto the page: it doesn't turn into a loose window, the page you were looking at comes back on screen and a card shows where the tab will go (the half of the pane on the side closest to the mouse). Drop it and the screen splits. You can put up to 4 tabs together, side by side, stacked or mixed (one on the left and two stacked on the right, for example). With 4, the card turns red.
- **A handle on each pane:** at the top middle of each split page there's a small line that turns into "•••" when the mouse gets close. Drag it to move the pane somewhere else, or to the sidebar to take it out of the split. A click activates the pane; right-click opens its menu (close, take out of the split, layouts).
- **Sizes:** drag the space between two panes to resize them. While you drag, the pages just slide (no lag), and adjust when you stop. Double-click makes the neighbors equal (with two tabs, it swaps them, as before).
- **Closing a tab doesn't undo the split:** the others stay together and the neighbor takes its place.
- **Menus and shortcuts:** in the tab menu, "Todas lado a lado" (all side by side), "Todas empilhadas" (all stacked), "Em grade" (grid), "Igualar os tamanhos" (equal sizes) and "Tirar esta aba da divisão" (take this tab out of the split); on a tab outside it, "Divisão à vista" → "Juntar à divisão" (join the split). Zen's shortcuts: Ctrl+Alt+V (side by side), Ctrl+Alt+H (stacked), Ctrl+Alt+G (grid) and Ctrl+Alt+U (undo). With tabs selected (Ctrl+click), it splits them.
- **In the sidebar,** the tabs of a split show up together on the same row.
- **Space between pages:** Settings → Aparência → "Espaço da tela dividida" (split view spacing), from 0 to 16 px. And the little bar with the address and the "x" in the corner of each split page is gone.
- **The split comes back as it was** when Norte reopens, with the sizes.
