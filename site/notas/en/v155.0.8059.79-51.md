- **Lucide icons across the whole browser:** the icons that still looked like Chrome (Material) are now Lucide, the same ones as the right-click menu: menus, the address bar and its suggestions, bubbles (site info, permissions, bookmark, downloads), find in page, media controls, window buttons and the internal pages (history, downloads, extensions). Where filling means something (bookmark star, pinned, liked, play/pause), the icon stays filled. The Chromium logo that showed up in a few places is now Norte's needle.
- **Hidden sidebar no longer opens by itself in the other window:** with two Norte windows, one on the left and one on the right, touching the edge of the right one with the pointer also opened the sidebar of the left one (on the same monitor or not). On Wayland, Chromium doesn't know where windows are, and the pointer position is the one in the window the pointer is in: the window losing focus read the other window's position (right at the edge) and thought the pointer was over its own sidebar. Now it checks that the pointer is actually in it. Also, "flinging the pointer into the corner of the screen" (which opens the sidebar like in Zen) only works at a real corner: it doesn't open when there's another monitor to the left, or when the pointer went into another window.
