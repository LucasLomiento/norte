First public version.

- Tabs in a sidebar that hides (Ctrl+S) and comes back when the pointer touches the edge.
- Spaces with their own tabs, pinned sites, folders and bookmarks; each space with its own container.
- Containers: separate cookies and logins within the same profile.
- Import from Zen: containers, spaces, pinned sites, each container's cookies, passwords, bookmarks and history.
- Downloads at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Updater: lets you know when a new version is out.
