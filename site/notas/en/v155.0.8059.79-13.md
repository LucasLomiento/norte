Turn it on and off in Settings → General (Geral) → Privacy (Privacidade); per site, from the site icon in the address bar (Intrusive ads). Customizable new tab (button in the corner: time, date and shortcuts — most visited, my shortcuts or none). Smaller menus (13 px). A lighter site-colored toolbar.
