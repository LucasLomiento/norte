- **Address bar without jumps on click:** before, pressing the mouse button on the bar moved the icon to the start and showed a blue outline; on release, the suggestions opened, the box grew a few pixels in every direction and the icon and text moved again. Now everything changes at once, on press: the suggestions open, the icon goes straight to its place (fading in, lined up with the suggestion icons) and the box keeps its size, with the list dropping below it. "Search Google or type a URL" stays until you type.
- **Clicking the bar selects the whole address:** if your hand shook a little before releasing, only part of the address could end up selected. Now a small shake doesn't count; a real drag still selects that part.
