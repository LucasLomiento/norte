- **Installed apps (PWA) with a container:** each installed app now has its own container, like spaces. When you install it, it stays in the container of the tab you installed it from (where you're logged in), and it always opens there, wherever it's launched from (launcher, link, Norte). To change it: the "⋮" menu of the app window → Container, or Norte Settings → Containers → Installed apps (Apps instalados). Changing it reloads the app's open windows in the new container, at the same address. Apps installed before this version stay without a container until you pick one.
