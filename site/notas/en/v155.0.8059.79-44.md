- **Sites stay logged in after updates:** Chromium was testing new formats for storing site data (IndexedDB and localStorage), and one step of that test deleted whatever was in the previous format. Now the format is locked. Before, a Norte update could log you out of WhatsApp Web or Discord.
- **No Google AI, for real:** pages no longer see the Gemini Nano APIs (summarize, write, proofread, chat) or WebMCP. The model isn't downloaded, tab contents aren't recorded for AI, and the AI Mode pop-up in the address bar, `@gemini` and the service that measured the graphics card for the model three minutes after startup are gone. Before, part of this was still on under the hood.
- **Lighter:** with the same 6 sites open, 17 processes instead of 18 and about 930 MB instead of 1,060. The window is also redrawn less often (sidebar, hidden bar, "now playing", the bar color when nothing changes).
- **Commands in the address bar:** `>` and the name of the action (`> foco`, `> trabalho`, `> container banco`, `> dividir`) show Norte's actions: the sidebar, the bar, spaces by name, containers, splitting the screen, closing duplicate tabs and the rest of the shortcuts. Enter runs the first one. Whatever you type after `>` isn't sent to the search engine's suggestions.
- **Calculator in the address bar:** a calculation (`2+3*4`, `15% de 80`, `2^10`, `(10-2)/3`) shows the result in the list, computed by Norte itself. Picking that row copies the result.
- **Links from other apps land in the right space:** in Edit space (Editar espaço) → Sites (`github.com, figma.com`), links to those sites coming from Discord, Slack or the terminal open in that space, and the window switches to it. `norte --space=<name> <address>` opens in the chosen space.
- **Style per site:** right-click the page → "Style for this site…" ("Estilo deste site…") turns on automatic dark mode just for that site and takes your own CSS, applied to all of its pages, on top of the site's CSS.
- **A Privacy page in Norte Settings (Privacidade):** the blocker, cookie notices, third-party cookies, the warning for sites without HTTPS, secure DNS and hiding your local IP in calls, all in one place.
- **Cookie notices hidden:** the EasyList Cookie list removes the consent notices of most sites. It can be turned off in Privacy.
- **Links without tracking:** right-click a link → "Copy clean link" ("Copiar link limpo", without `utm`, `fbclid` and the like) and "Copy link as Markdown" ("Copiar link como Markdown"). `Ctrl+Shift+C` also copies the tab's address without the tracking parameters.
- **Blocker per site on right-click:** "Turn off the blocker on this site" ("Desligar o bloqueador neste site", and "Ligar" to turn it back on) in the page menu. Before, only through the site information.
- **Containers:** "New temporary container" ("Novo container temporário") in the container menus opens the tab in a gray container that disappears, with its cookies and everything else, when its last tab closes. And notifications work inside a container: WhatsApp Web, Gmail and Calendar opened in a container now show notifications, and clicking them takes you to the site. Before, Chromium denied every notification inside a container.
- **Follower tab:** with the screen split, tab menu → Arrange split view → "Links from this tab open in the other half" ("Links desta aba abrem na outra metade"). Read a list on one side and open the pages on the other.
- **New tab notice:** when a link opens in a new tab (a link that opens a new tab, Ctrl+click, middle click), its row in the sidebar lights up and fades out in under a second. With the sidebar hidden, a pill at the bottom of the page says "Opened in a new tab" ("Aberta numa aba nova"); clicking it goes to the tab.
- **Sidebar:** with many tabs, when the active tab leaves the visible part of the list, a notice at the top or bottom shows which one it is; clicking it scrolls there. With the sidebar and the bar hidden, a thin line pulses at the top of the page while it loads.
- **Bring things over from Chrome, Brave or Vivaldi, and take Norte to another machine:** in General (Geral) → Bring from another browser (Trazer de outro navegador), the bookmarks and history of those browsers, plus Export/Import of Norte's settings (spaces, pinned sites, favorites, containers, shortcuts and options) in a file.
- **New shortcuts:** `Ctrl+Alt+C` hides the sidebar and the bar together (focus mode), `Ctrl+Alt+6` to `9` go to spaces 6 to 9, and renaming the tab, pinning, adding to favorites and closing the space get a shortcut if you pick one. "Ctrl+Tab in order of use" ("Ctrl+Tab na ordem de uso") became an option in Shortcuts (Atalhos).
- **More at the bottom and in pinned sites:** right-clicking the bottom of the sidebar closes duplicate tabs, copies the tabs' addresses and unloads the other spaces. With the pointer on the line between pinned sites and tabs, "Clear" ("Limpar") closes the space's tabs. On a pinned site that moved to another page, "Back to the pinned address" ("Voltar ao endereço fixado").
- **Look:**
  - the sidebar over the page has the chosen opacity and a shadow;
  - the bar color changes together with the tab and fades when switching spaces;
  - hover shows on the bar buttons and on the tabs' X;
  - secondary buttons and switches are gray now, and checked boxes are blue (before, the check mark disappeared on them);
  - the menus got Lucide icons;
  - the address bar suggestions lost the little blue bar, and the Chromium actions suggested there show Norte's needle instead of the Chromium logo;
  - the Shift+click preview grows from the point of the click;
  - the hidden bar got a shadow;
  - the space dots grow under the pointer, and favorites were redone;
  - light mode has its own palette;
  - the logo and downloads line up with the tab icons.
- **New options, off by default:** freeze the tabs of other spaces after 15 minutes (General → Memory, Geral → Memória), scroll the page with the middle button (General → Mouse) and the video in a small window when switching tabs (General → Media, Geral → Mídia).
- **Updates:** the new version notice has "What changed" ("O que mudou"), and the check runs every 6 hours while Norte is open, not only at startup.
- **For tinkerers:** errors of Norte opened from the menu go to `journalctl -t norte`. The blocker also works with `--disable-component-update`. `--norte-mv2` in `norte-flags.conf` allows MV2 extensions, with no guarantee.
