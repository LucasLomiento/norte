- **No longer stuck unable to open after a crash:** with the address bar hidden (Ctrl+Shift+S), if Norte crashed once, it crashed again on every start and never opened. After a crash, the "Restore pages?" bubble looked for a spot to show up that didn't exist yet with the bar hidden. Now it shows up in the top corner, and you can restore your tabs.
- **One less crash when dragging tabs between windows:** while putting tabs that arrived from another window into the space, Norte could ask for a tab group Chromium had already closed midway, and crashed (it happened on KDE, right after a new window was born from a drag). Now the tabs go one at a time, checking each one's group first.
- **Memória: Norte remembers what you read.** The text of the pages you read is kept on this computer, and you find them again by what you remember (a phrase, a term, even in Japanese), not by the address. Ctrl+Shift+F opens the panel over the page: type, and the pages show up with the passage and the words highlighted, a thumbnail of how you saw it and when. Enter opens the page already scrolled to the passage, highlighted; if it's already open, it goes to that tab. With nothing typed, a timeline of what you read today, yesterday, this week.
- **In the address bar too:** what you type also finds the text of pages you read, with the passage next to the title; picking it opens at the passage. "Buscar … na Memória" (search in Memória) opens the panel with your text.
- **No AI, and only here:** it's word search, in a file in your profile. Left out: incognito windows, pages with a password field, banks and email (they ask not to be stored), email and chat apps, search results pages and whatever you remove: right-click a page → "Não lembrar deste site" (don't remember this site), or Settings → Memória (sites, containers, how long to keep, delete everything). Deleting history deletes the same pages here.
