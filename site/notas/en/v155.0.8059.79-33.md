- **A frozen screen now recovers on its own:** with the new NVIDIA driver on Hyprland, the window sometimes stopped redrawing (the browser was still alive behind it). Now, if you're using the window and it goes 5 s without redrawing, Norte restarts its graphics process by itself, with your tabs and videos right where they were. This happens at most once every 5 minutes.
- **"Now playing" at the bottom of the sidebar:** music or video from another tab, even one in another space, shows up in a card with the artwork, the title, play/pause, next track and a progress bar (click to skip ahead). Clicking the card takes you to the tab. Chromium's media button is gone from the toolbar.
- **Link preview with Shift + click:** the page opens in a card over the current tab, with the background dimmed and blurred. Esc, clicking outside or the "x" closes it. "Open as tab" ("Abrir como aba") and "Split" ("Dividir") turn the preview into a tab without reloading. The preview opens in the same container as the tab it came from. You can turn it off in Settings → General (Geral) → Links.
- **Ctrl+Shift+C copies the tab's address,** with a "Link copied" notice. DevTools is still on F12 and Ctrl+Shift+I, and you can change the key in Settings → Shortcuts (Atalhos).
- **"Close this space's tabs"** ("Fechar as abas deste espaço") in the space name menu and when you right-click the dots. Pinned sites stay, and Ctrl+Shift+T brings the tabs back.
- **Thin scrollbars** over the page, which only show up while you scroll.
- **Animated space switching:** the tab list slides sideways.
