- **The private window's new tab in Norte's design:** an outlined compass slowly turning in the middle of the page and, in three columns, what the window forgets when you close it (history, cookies, logins and whatever you typed into forms), what it keeps (downloads and bookmarks) and who can still see what you visit (the sites, your network and your internet provider).
- **Guest mode with the same look:** the guest's new tab explains that whoever uses the window can't see your profile (bookmarks, passwords, history, spaces and extensions) and that nothing stays once it's closed.
