- **Space switching fixed.** After Chromium 157, going back to a space could leave the old name at the top of the sidebar and hide its pinned folders.
- **The sidebar moves smoothly again.** With `Ctrl+S`, the page resizes along with the sidebar instead of jumping.
- **Drag pinned sites out.** Drop a pinned site or favorite on the tab list to turn it into a regular tab, right where the line shows. If it wasn't open, the tab opens in the background.
