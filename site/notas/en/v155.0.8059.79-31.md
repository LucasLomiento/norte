- **Private window without spaces, containers or pinned sites:** it's now a regular window, with Norte's sidebar and just the tabs. The header says "Anônima" (or "Visitante" for guests), tabs aren't grouped, the tab menu has the usual "Pin", space shortcuts go to the page, and closing the last tab closes the window. Nothing from it is saved to your profile.
- **Pinned sites and bookmarks scroll:** with lots of pinned sites or bookmarks, the area at the top took over the whole sidebar and the tab list disappeared. Now that area scrolls with the wheel and the tabs stay visible below.
- **No duplicate bookmarks:** pinning a site that's already in your bookmarks no longer creates another identical tile. Extensions that pin tabs (like Claude in Chrome) created a duplicate tile every time.
- **"Norte" in more places:** the private window's new tab, the crashed-tab page, certificate warnings, passwords and autofill still said "Chromium".
- **Shortcuts:** you can no longer record a shortcut with only Shift (Shift+A stole the capital "A" from everything you typed on web pages): it needs Ctrl, Alt or Super.
- **Safer address bar:** the page title shown next to the domain can no longer use text-direction control characters to imitate a different address.
- **Downloads:** the button's animation no longer wastes resources while the sidebar is hidden or the window is minimized.
