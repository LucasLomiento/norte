- **Claude in Chrome works:** the tab group the extension creates becomes a temporary space, which goes away on its own when the group closes (before, the extension failed with "No group with id").
- **Two crashes fixed:** moving the only tab of a space, and "Open in container" on a tab inside a group, used to bring Norte down.
- **Tab menu:** "Open in container", "Reload" and "Duplicate" sometimes did something else (move to a space, pin). Now every item does what it says.
- **Containers:** links that open in a new tab (`target=_blank`) stay in the tab's container; the private window doesn't use containers; "Clear browsing data" and deleting a container also erase the containers' data; a tab discarded to save memory comes back with its container, name and pinned state.
- **Spaces:** tabs in other spaces are no longer frozen; the touchpad switches one space per gesture; popups, PWAs and DevTools close along with the last tab.
- **Hidden address bar:** stays visible while a menu or bubble is open and while the bookmarks have focus; `Ctrl+K` shows the bar.
- **Safer updater:** downloads into a private folder and checks the package's SHA-256 before installing.
- **The right default browser:** when opened from a link in an Electron app (Resenha, Vesktop, Slack), Norte registered itself under that app's name, and "set as default" could leave the other app as the system browser. If your links started opening in the wrong place, set Norte as the default again after updating.
