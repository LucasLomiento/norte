- **Your container logins are better protected.** An internal Chromium cleanup, which runs after uninstalling certain apps or extensions, could wipe the cookies of every container that had no tab open. It no longer touches them.
- **Deleting a container asks for confirmation**, and tabs open in it reopen outside the container instead of staying somewhere nothing else would ever clean.
- **Memory: what you delete stays deleted.** A page you told Norte to forget came back on its own when you switched tabs with its tab still open; deleting an item from history now removes the page from Memory; and the words of deleted pages are really removed from the file.
- **Memory no longer stops remembering after Back.** On sites like GitHub and YouTube, going back a page made the tab stop being remembered until a reload.
- **Pages open a little faster with the blocker on.** The rules that hide ads were parsed again on every page; now they are kept.
- **"Unload the other spaces" no longer drops a call or loses what you typed**: tabs in a call, sharing the screen or with a filled-in form are kept.
- **Many spaces fit in the sidebar footer**: with 7 or more, the dots become a row around the current space and the "+" stays visible.
- **Address bar calculator**: `1,000 + 2` gives 1002 in English (`2.500 * 3` gives 7500 in Portuguese), and `0x10` or a date like `10/21/2026` is not treated as a sum.
- **Details**: the downloads icon follows the light/dark switch; Settings buttons are visible in dark mode; after renaming a tab the keyboard goes back to the page; `>` commands stay together in the bar; "Open link in container" no longer breaks the split view; and the sidebar right-click menu lost items that made no sense in Norte.
